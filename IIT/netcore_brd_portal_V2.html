⚠️ Email + Aggressive Pacing Risk
🚨
You selected Email with Aggressive warmup
Aggressive pacing gives only 5 days of email warmup. For email channels this is significantly below the industry standard of 14–21 days and may result in:
📉 High bounce rates
ISPs reject mail from cold domains sending at volume
🚫 Spam placement
Emails land in spam folder instead of inbox
📌 Domain reputation
Permanent damage to sending domain reputation
🔒 Account suspension
ESP may suspend account for policy violation
Recommendation: Only proceed with Aggressive if this sending domain is already actively warmed up and has a strong sending reputation. Otherwise switch to Moderate (10d warmup) or Normal (14d warmup).
